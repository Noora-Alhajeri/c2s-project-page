Abstract

{{PROJECT_ABSTRACT}}

Problem
Single-path VLA control is slow and compute heavy for repeated tasks.
Core Idea
Novelty-gated transfer from conscious planning to subconscious execution.
Contribution
Triune semantic-episodic-procedural architecture with continuous monitoring.

Motivation

How can a humanoid keep conscious and subconscious control active at the same time?

How can it plan long-term goals while reacting to fast sensor feedback?

How does conscious effort become subconscious skill after practice?

Method

Three Brain Layers
  • Neocortex: scene understanding, symbolic planning, rationale.
  • Limbic: episodic retrieval, novelty and recall-based gating.
  • Reptilian: high-frequency policy execution and control.
Control Loop
  • Semantic monitor remains active during procedural mode.
  • Novelty threshold selects conscious vs subconscious route.
  • Unexpected error triggers monitor intervention and replanning.

Key Results

Performance
  • Conscious-to-subconscious speedup after repeated practice.
  • Novelty decay and learning curves tracked per episode.
  • Closed-loop monitoring remains active in procedural phase.
Evidence
  • Ablation, zero-shot transfer, and failure taxonomy.
  • Conscious vs subconscious statistics with significance tests.
  • Bundle-ready plots, videos, and LaTeX tables.
